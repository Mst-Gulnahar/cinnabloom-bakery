## 🍓 Welcome to Cinnabloom!

Cinnabloom Bakery is a cozy, full-stack artisan bakery web application built with a warm folk-art aesthetic. It delivers fresh cinnamon rolls straight from our oven to your digital doorstep, complete with interactive customization, community recipes, secure ordering, and **dual agentic AI features** powered by the Gemini API!

---

## 🍯 What We Provide on This Website

* **Cozy Flavor Library:** Browse our delicious catalog of freshly baked rolls, sticky buns, and seasonal sweet treats with live category filtering and search.
* **Interactive Roll Customizer:** Design your dream cinnamon roll by picking custom frostings (like Salted Bourbon Caramel or Uji Matcha Drizzle) and extra toppings, then add them directly to your bag.
* **Daily Hot Drops Schedule:** Keep track of our staggered baking schedule so you can grab your rolls piping hot out of the oven.
* **Daily Scratch & Win Offers:** Reveal secret promo codes and discounts with our interactive daily bakery perk scratcher.
* **Catering & Party Boxes:** Explore custom party trays and multi-piece roll towers tailored for birthdays, office meetings, and special events.
* **Community Raves & FAQ:** Read genuine reviews from verified dessert lovers and find answers to all your storage and freshness questions.
* **User Accounts & Secure Cart:** Seamlessly log in, manage your shopping bag, apply discounts, and complete your bakery orders.

---

## 🤖 Powered by Gemini AI Integration

This project features **two dedicated Agentic AI capabilities** built using the Gemini API to elevate the baking and ordering experience:

1. **AI Description Maker (Content Generator):** 
   * Automatically generates rich, mouth-watering product descriptions, flavor notes, and marketing copy for bakery items using custom prompt structures and adjustable parameters.
2. **AI Delivery Man (Rider Chat Assistant):** 
   * An interactive context-aware chatbot companion ("The Delivery Rider") embedded in the app. It assists users with live order tracking, delivery estimates, menu questions, and friendly conversational support with full chat history memory.

## 🥞 Tech Stack Highlights

* **Frontend:** Next.js, React.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Lucide Icons
* **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js, TypeScript, MongoDB, Secure Authentication
* **AI Engine:** Google Gemini API (supporting content generation and conversational agent workflows)
